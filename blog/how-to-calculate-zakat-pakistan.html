How to Calculate Zakat in Pakistan (2026 Guide)

A clear, step-by-step handbook covering Nisab thresholds in tolas and grams, bank savings deductions, commercial inventory, and deductible debts according to authentic Islamic rules.

Zakat is the third pillar of Islam, acting as both a compulsory spiritual duty and an economic mechanism designed to circulate wealth and uplift vulnerable members of society. In Pakistan, calculating Zakat has traditionally been a seasonal family responsibility around the month of Ramadan, when individuals tally up their savings, family gold jewelry, and commercial assets to determine their annual 2.5% contribution.

However, navigating fluctuating gold rates in Karachi and Lahore Sarafa bazaars, understanding bank deduction notices from the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP), and separating deductible personal debts from taxable assets often creates confusion. This comprehensive 2026 guide breaks down every element in straightforward English, so you can calculate your obligations with complete peace of mind.

What is Nisab and How is it Determined in Pakistan?

Before you are obligated to pay Zakat, your qualifying net wealth must equal or exceed a minimum threshold called the Nisab. If your net assets fall below this threshold for the lunar year, you are not required to pay Zakat, and you may even be eligible to receive it if you are in need.

The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) established two distinct benchmarks for Nisab:

  • Gold Nisab: 20 Mithqals of gold, which equals exactly 7.5 Tolas in traditional Pakistani units (or 87.48 grams).
  • Silver Nisab: 200 Dirhams of silver, which equals exactly 52.5 Tolas in Pakistani units (or 612.36 grams).
Which Nisab standard should you follow in Pakistan? Contemporary scholars and Islamic jurisprudence councils unanimously advise that if you possess mixed wealth (such as cash, bank savings, business inventory, prize bonds, or silver alongside gold), you should base your calculation on the Silver Nisab. Because the monetary value of 52.5 tolas of silver is significantly lower than 7.5 tolas of gold, using the silver standard allows more wealth to be purified and benefits a greater number of deserving families (mustahiqeen).

The One Lunar Year Requirement (Hawl)

Zakat is not an income tax; it is a wealth tax. You only pay Zakat on assets that have remained in your possession for one complete Islamic lunar year (a Hawl, which spans approximately 354 days). Once your net wealth crosses the Nisab threshold on a specific lunar date (for instance, the 1st of Ramadan), that becomes your personal Zakat anniversary date. If your total net wealth remains equal to or above the Nisab at the completion of that lunar year, you calculate 2.5% on your total qualifying balance on that day.

Step-by-Step Breakdown: What Assets are Zakat-Eligible?

To calculate your Zakat accurately, compile all qualifying assets owned on your valuation date:

1. Gold and Silver

All physical gold and silver in your possession must be evaluated at current local market bullion rates on your calculation day. Under the Hanafi school of jurisprudence followed by the vast majority of Muslims in Pakistan, personal jewelry worn by women is subject to Zakat if the Nisab threshold is reached. Note that you calculate Zakat only on the net weight of pure gold or silver, excluding stones, diamonds, or non-precious embellishments.

2. Cash in Hand and Bank Accounts

Include all liquidity across:

  • Physical cash kept at home or in lockers.
  • Current bank accounts, profit & loss sharing (PLS) savings accounts, and term deposit receipts (TDRs).
  • Balances stored in Pakistani digital fintech wallets such as Easypaisa, JazzCash, Nayapay, and Sadapay.
  • Foreign currency cash holdings (USD, SAR, AED, GBP, EUR) converted to Pakistani Rupees at the prevailing open market exchange rate.

3. Business Stock in Trade (Maal-e-Tijarat)

If you operate a retail shop, wholesale business, or online store in Pakistan, all finished goods and inventory held for the express purpose of sale are subject to Zakat. Value this stock at its current wholesale replacement cost on your calculation date, not the original manufacturing or retail selling price. Raw materials intended for finished goods are also included.

4. Prize Bonds, Shares, and Mutual Funds

National Prize Bonds issued by the Central Directorate of National Savings are fully cash-equivalent and must be included at 100% of their face value. For shares of publicly traded companies on the Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX), if you bought shares for short-term trading/capital gains, pay Zakat on the full market value. If held for long-term dividend income, consult your fund manager to calculate Zakat solely on the company's liquid and inventory assets.

What Wealth is Strictly EXEMPT from Zakat?

Islamic law grants generous exemptions for personal necessities and productive tools that sustain your daily livelihood:

Asset Category Zakat Status Reason / Explanation
Personal Residence 100% Exempt The house or apartment you live in is a basic necessity (Hajat-e-Asliyyah).
Personal Vehicles 100% Exempt Cars, motorcycles, and vans used for personal or family commute.
Household Goods 100% Exempt Furniture, home appliances, air conditioners, clothing, and computers.
Business Fixtures & Machinery 100% Exempt Factory machinery, delivery vehicles, shop furniture, and computer hardware.
Agricultural Land & Production Exempt from Zakat Subject to Ushr (produce tax at harvest time) rather than wealth Zakat.
Rental Property Value Building is Exempt Pay Zakat only on accumulated net rental cash income, not the property itself.

Deductible Liabilities: What Can You Subtract?

Before applying the 2.5% rate, you are entitled to subtract your immediate, short-term liabilities from your total wealth pool. Allowable deductions include:

  • Pending utility bills (such as your current month's electricity bill, gas, and water).
  • Immediate house or shop rent due for payment.
  • Overdue salaries payable to household staff, maids, or business employees.
  • Immediate debts and supplier invoices due within the current monthly cycle.
  • For long-term commercial loans or home mortgages, you may only deduct the installments due within the immediate upcoming 12 months, not the total multi-year balance.

The Mathematical Formula for Your Zakat

Once you have summarized your figures, the formula is remarkably simple:

Net Zakat-Eligible Wealth = (Total Gold & Silver Value + Cash & Bank Balances + Business Stock + Prize Bonds) - (Immediate Debts & Expenses)

If your Net Wealth is greater than the current Silver Nisab value in Pakistani Rupees, your payable Zakat is:

Payable Zakat = Net Zakat-Eligible Wealth × 0.025 (or divided by 40)

For example, if your net qualifying assets equal Rs. 1,200,000, your annual Zakat obligation is Rs. 30,000.

Pakistani Bank Zakat Deductions (CZ-50 Exemption)

Under the Zakat and Ushr Ordinance of 1980, commercial banks in Pakistan automatically deduct 2.5% Zakat on the 1st of Ramadan from PLS savings accounts, profit-bearing accounts, and term deposits exceeding the government-notified Nisab. Current checking accounts are legally exempt from automatic deduction.

If you prefer to distribute your Zakat directly to needy relatives, reputable grassroots charities (such as Edhi Foundation, Indus Hospital, Saylani Welfare, or Shaukat Khanum), or local families in your neighborhood, you can submit a notarized CZ-50 Declaration Form (Zakat Exemption Affidavit) on Rs. 50 stamp paper to your bank branch at least 30 days before Ramadan. This prevents the bank from auto-deducting Zakat, leaving you free to disburse it yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

🪙 Should I calculate using Gold or Silver Nisab in Pakistan?

If you only possess gold and zero other assets, you can use the Gold Nisab (7.5 tolas). However, if you hold any cash, savings, silver, or business stock, mainstream Pakistani scholars instruct you to use the Silver Nisab (52.5 tolas) to ensure maximum benefit for deserving beneficiaries.

💍 Do women have to pay Zakat on everyday gold jewelry?

According to the Hanafi school of jurisprudence followed widely in Pakistan, yes. All gold and silver jewelry, regardless of whether it is worn daily or stored in a bank locker, is subject to Zakat if the owner's total wealth meets the Nisab.

🤝 Can I give Zakat to my close relatives in Pakistan?

You cannot give Zakat to your direct ascendants (parents, grandparents) or direct descendants (children, grandchildren), nor to your spouse. However, giving Zakat to needy siblings (brothers, sisters), uncles, aunts, cousins, and in-laws is not only permissible but carries double spiritual reward — one for charity and one for upholding family ties.

💼 Is Zakat payable on Provident Fund (GP Fund) and Gratuity?

According to the majority of contemporary Pakistani scholars, Zakat is not due on compulsory provident funds as long as the funds remain held by your employer. Once you retire and the money is physically credited to your personal account, it becomes subject to Zakat after completing one lunar year.
