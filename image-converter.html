❓ Image Converter FAQ

🔄 What happens to transparency when converting PNG to JPG?

Because the standard JPEG format does not support alpha channel transparency, any transparent areas in your PNG are smoothly rendered over a crisp white background.

🌐 Why should I convert images to WebP?

WebP images are on average 25% to 35% smaller than comparable JPEGs and PNGs while retaining crisp quality and transparency support, speeding up website loading times.
