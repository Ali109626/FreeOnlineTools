❓ Generators FAQ

🔑 Are passwords created here secure?

Yes, they are generated using `window.crypto.getRandomValues()`, the gold standard for cryptographic pseudo-random number generation in modern browsers. The passwords are never saved, logged, or sent over any network.

📱 Do the generated QR codes ever expire?

No! They are static QR codes containing raw text or URLs directly encoded into the visual pattern. They work forever with zero redirects and zero expiration.
