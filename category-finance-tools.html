❓ Finance Tools FAQ

🔒 Are my financial figures private?

Yes, 100%. All computations execute client-side directly in your browser. None of your salary figures, bank loans, or savings numbers are transmitted to any server or recorded in any database.

🏛️ Are the Pakistan salary tax slabs up to date?

Yes. The salary tax calculator is configured with the latest FBR progressive tax slabs enacted under the Finance Act for the 2026-27 fiscal year, and includes an editable slab panel for mini-budget updates.

📊 How accurate are the loan EMI calculations?

The loan calculator uses the universal standard reducing-balance amortization formula used by banks globally and in Pakistan (KIBOR plus spread), delivering exact figures.
