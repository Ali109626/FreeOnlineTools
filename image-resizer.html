❓ Image Resizer FAQ

📐 What does "Maintain Aspect Ratio" mean?

Maintaining aspect ratio preserves the proportional relationship between the image width and height so your photo doesn't look squished, stretched, or deformed.

🔒 Can anyone see the pictures I resize?

Never. Your image data stays 100% within your local device memory. Nothing is transmitted across any network.
