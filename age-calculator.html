❓ Age Calculator FAQ

🎂 Does this calculator account for leap years?

Yes, our date engine accurately accounts for Gregorian leap years (February 29) to calculate the precise number of days, weeks, and months elapsed.

📅 Can I find my age at a historical event?

Yes! Simply set the "Target Date" to any historical date in the past to calculate exactly how old you were at that specific moment.
