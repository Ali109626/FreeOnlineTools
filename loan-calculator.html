❓ Loan EMI Calculator Frequently Asked Questions

💡 What does EMI stand for?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. It is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month until the principal and interest are paid in full.

📉 How does early repayment affect my loan?

Making early principal prepayments reduces the outstanding balance faster, which shortens your overall loan tenure and dramatically decreases total interest paid.

🏠 Can I use this for home mortgages and auto finance in Pakistan?

Yes, this calculator works with all Pakistani commercial bank financing products (HBL, Meezan, Bank Alfalah, MCB, UBL) for auto leasing, home loans, and solar financing.

📊 Why is interest higher in the initial years?

Under the standard reducing-balance method, interest is calculated on the remaining loan principal. Because the principal is highest in the initial years, interest constitutes a larger portion of early EMI payments.
