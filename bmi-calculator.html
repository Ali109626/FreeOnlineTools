❓ BMI Calculator FAQ

⚖️ What are the standard World Health Organization (WHO) BMI ranges?

• Underweight: Under 18.5
• Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
• Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
• Obese: 30.0 or greater

🏃 Does BMI distinguish between muscle mass and fat?

BMI is a screening tool based on height and weight. Highly trained athletes or bodybuilders with substantial muscle mass may have a high BMI without excess body fat.
