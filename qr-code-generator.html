❓ QR Code Generator FAQ

📱 Will these QR codes expire after a few months?

No, never! These are genuine static QR codes. The destination URL or WiFi details are directly encoded into the visual black-and-white grid pattern without any intermediate tracking servers or expiration timers.

📶 How does a WiFi QR code work?

When visitors or customers scan your WiFi QR code using their phone camera, their device automatically connects to the wireless network without having to type out long, complex passwords.
