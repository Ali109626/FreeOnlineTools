❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FreeOnlineTools.

🔒 Are my files and photos uploaded to your server?

No, absolutely not! FreeOnlineTools operates 100% client-side using modern HTML5, Canvas, and JavaScript APIs. Your images, PDFs, and text stay right on your phone or computer memory and never leave your device.

💸 Is there any fee, subscription, or watermark?

Every single tool on FreeOnlineTools is completely free to use forever. There are no subscriptions, no premium tiers, no watermarks, and no sign-up or login required.

📱 Does this website work well on budget Android phones?

Yes! We built FreeOnlineTools with a mobile-first philosophy. The pages are lightweight, without heavy framework overhead, ensuring fast loading even on 3G/4G connections and low-cost smartphones.

⚡ How accurate is the Pakistan Electricity Bill Calculator?

Our calculator provides an accurate estimate based on standard slab structures (protected and unprotected consumers) and includes Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA), GST, TV fee, and surcharges. Furthermore, all tariff rates are fully editable so you can customize them to match your exact DISCO bill!

🌐 Can I use these tools offline?

Once the page has loaded in your browser, virtually all tools (including image compression, calculations, case conversion, and unit conversions) function even without an active internet connection!
