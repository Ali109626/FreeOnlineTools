❓ Compound Interest Frequently Asked Questions

📈 What is compound interest?

Compound interest is the interest on your original deposit plus the interest that has accumulated from previous periods. In simple terms, it is "interest earning interest", creating an exponential snowball effect.

⏳ What is the Rule of 72?

The Rule of 72 is a quick shortcut to estimate how many years it takes to double your investment. Divide 72 by your annual interest rate. For example, at a 12% return, your money doubles in approximately 6 years (72 ÷ 12 = 6).

🏦 Does compounding frequency make a big difference?

More frequent compounding (such as monthly vs annually) yields slightly higher returns because profits are credited and begin earning interest earlier in the cycle.

🎯 Why are monthly contributions so powerful?

Consistent monthly contributions smooth out market volatility through dollar-cost averaging and continually expand the base of capital working for you.
