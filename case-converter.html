❓ Case Converter FAQ

🔠 What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalized Case?

Title Case capitalizes the principal words while keeping minor grammatical conjunctions and prepositions (like "and", "in", "the") in lowercase unless they begin the sentence.

💻 What are camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case used for?

These formats are widely used by software developers, programmers, and SEO specialists for variable names, JSON keys, CSS class identifiers, and web URL slugs.
