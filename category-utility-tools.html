❓ Utility Tools FAQ

💾 Will I lose my To-Do tasks if I close the tab?

No! Your tasks are automatically saved in your browser's persistent `localStorage`. When you revisit the page on the same device and browser, your to-dos will remain intact.

💱 Can I adjust the exchange rates in the Currency Converter?

Yes, we provide standard reference rates, but exchange rates fluctuate constantly in Pakistan's open and interbank markets. You can freely edit any currency rate directly in the table to get an exact calculation.
