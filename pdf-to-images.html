❓ PDF to Images Frequently Asked Questions

🖼️ Which format should I choose, JPG or PNG?

Choose JPG if your PDF contains photos, scanned books, or magazines, as JPG files are much smaller. Choose PNG if your PDF contains fine technical diagrams, invoices, logos, or tiny text that needs crisp sharpness.

🔒 Are my files uploaded to an external server?

No. Conversion runs 100% inside your web browser using Mozilla's pdf.js library. Your PDF never leaves your device, keeping your financial, legal, and personal files completely confidential.

📱 Can I convert PDF to JPG on Android or iPhone?

Yes! You can convert any PDF on modern mobile phones and tablets, preview every single page thumbnail, and save them straight to your phone's photo gallery.

📦 Can I download all pages at once?

Yes, tapping "Download All Pages" will automatically prompt your browser to save each converted page image sequentially into your downloads folder.
