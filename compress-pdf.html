❓ Compress PDF Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Are my sensitive documents safe from data leaks?

Absolutely. Unlike other cloud converter websites that transmit your confidential documents to external servers, this tool works 100% locally in your web browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

📉 How much smaller will my PDF become?

Digital text PDFs usually see 20% to 45% reduction via object stream optimization, while scanned documents containing high-resolution images can be compressed by up to 60% to 80% without unreadable text.

🎓 Is this suitable for FPSC, PPSC, and university admissions?

Yes! Pakistani online testing services, FPSC, PPSC, and educational portals require uploaded documents to be under 500 KB or 1 MB. This tool is calibrated specifically for those limits.

📱 Can I compress PDFs on a mobile phone?

Yes, our responsive mobile-first interface works seamlessly in Google Chrome, Safari, and Samsung Internet on Android and iOS devices.
