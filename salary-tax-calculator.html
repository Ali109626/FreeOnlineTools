❓ Pakistan Salary Tax Frequently Asked Questions

💵 What is the minimum salary exempt from tax in Pakistan?

Under the FBR tax schedule, individuals earning up to Rs. 600,000 per year (equivalent to Rs. 50,000 per month) pay 0% income tax.

🏛️ Who qualifies for Salaried Individual tax slabs?

If your salary income constitutes more than 75% of your total taxable income for the tax year, you are taxed under the Salaried Individual schedule, which features lower rates than business or freelance individuals.

📈 How does progressive tax work in Pakistan?

Progressive tax means you only pay higher rates on the portion of income that exceeds each bracket threshold. For example, if you earn Rs. 1,000,000 a year, the first Rs. 600,000 is taxed at 0%, and only the remaining Rs. 400,000 is taxed at 5%.

📑 Can I claim tax credits for donations or health insurance?

Yes, filers can claim tax rebates on donations made to approved charitable organizations (under clause 61 of Part I of Second Schedule) and approved voluntary pension schemes (VPS) when filing annual returns.
