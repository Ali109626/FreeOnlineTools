❓ Image Tools FAQ

🔒 Is it safe to compress or convert personal photos here?

Yes, 100%! All image manipulation occurs entirely inside your browser's local HTML5 Canvas memory. We do not have an image upload server; your private photos never touch the internet.

📸 What is the best image format for website speed?

WebP is widely considered the best modern format because it delivers superior lossless and lossy compression compared to traditional JPEG and PNG formats, loading faster on mobile phones.
