❓ Pakistan Electricity Bill FAQ

⚡ What is the difference between Protected and Unprotected consumers?

Protected consumers are residential households consuming 200 units or less per month consistently for the last 6 consecutive months. They enjoy heavily subsidized government tariff slabs. If consumption exceeds 200 units in even one month, the consumer is classified as unprotected and charged higher standard tariffs.

💰 What is Fuel Price Adjustment (FPA)?

FPA is a variable surcharge approved by NEPRA reflecting fluctuations in fuel prices (coal, furnace oil, RLNG) used by power generation companies compared to benchmark reference tariffs.

📝 Can I use this for K-Electric Karachi?

Yes, select "K-Electric" in the distributor dropdown. You can adjust the slab inputs to reflect NEPRA's multi-year tariff for KE.
