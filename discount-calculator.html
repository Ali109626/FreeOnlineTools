❓ Discount Calculator FAQ

🏷️ How is a percentage discount calculated?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage divided by 100 to get savings amount, then subtract that from the original price.

📈 How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100.
