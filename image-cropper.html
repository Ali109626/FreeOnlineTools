❓ Image Cropper FAQ

📱 What is the best aspect ratio for social media profile pictures?

Use the 1:1 (Square) preset. Most platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn) use square avatars that get masked into circles.

🔒 Is my cropped photo stored anywhere?

No. All cropping and canvas slicing occurs inside your browser memory. Nothing is ever saved to a server.
