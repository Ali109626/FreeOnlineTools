❓ Word Counter FAQ

📝 What is the character limit for Twitter / X?

Twitter/X allows 280 characters for standard accounts. Our character counter tracks this with and without spaces so you can stay within your limits.

⏱️ How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated using the standard average reading speed of 200 words per minute (wpm) for English and Urdu text.
