❓ Savings Goal Planner Frequently Asked Questions

🎯 How should I set an emergency fund target?

Financial advisors recommend keeping 3 to 6 months of mandatory household living expenses (food, rent, utility bills, school fees) in an easily accessible liquid account.

📈 What return rate should I enter if saving in cash?

If you are keeping cash at home or in a non-profit checking account, set the return rate to 0%. If placing funds in an Islamic mutual fund or PLS savings account, enter 9% to 14%.

💡 What if the required monthly savings is too high for my salary?

You can easily extend your timeline (e.g. from 12 months to 24 months) or adjust your target goal downward to find a realistic monthly commitment that fits your family budget.

🤝 Is this calculation private?

Yes, 100%. All computations execute client-side in your web browser. None of your personal financial figures or savings targets are stored or transmitted anywhere.
