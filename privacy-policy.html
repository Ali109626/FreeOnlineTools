Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Your privacy is our highest technical commitment.

🔒 Fundamental Architecture: Zero Server Uploads

1. Introduction

At FreeOnlineTools ("we", "us", or "our"), reachable at freeonlinetools.vercel.app, we believe that your private photos, confidential PDF documents, text files, and personal calculations belong solely to you. This Privacy Policy details our operational protocols, data practices, and commitment to transparency.

2. 100% Client-Side Processing (No Files Stored)

Unlike traditional online conversion utilities that upload your files to remote cloud servers for background rendering, FreeOnlineTools executes every single tool directly inside your client browser using modern web technologies (HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript). When you compress an image, merge PDF files, convert Roman Urdu, or calculate an electricity bill:

  • Your files are never transmitted across the internet to our servers.
  • Your files are never stored, logged, inspected, or retained by us.
  • When you close your browser tab, all temporary memory allocations are discarded automatically.

3. Local Storage

Certain tools (such as our To-Do List utility) make use of your browser's native `localStorage` feature. This data resides strictly on your physical device hardware and is never sent back to any network or server. You have full control over this data and may clear it at any time by wiping your browser history or storage.

4. Advertising & Google AdSense

We may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense and third-party advertising networks to keep all tools free for everyone.

  • Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Log Files & Analytics

Like standard hosting infrastructure (such as Vercel), standard non-personally identifiable server logs (such as IP addresses, browser types, internet service providers, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps) may be recorded for operational security, performance monitoring, and anti-abuse purposes. These logs are not linked to any personally identifiable information.

6. Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. Because our website requires no user account, login, or age declaration, we collect zero personal databases.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or inquiries regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page or email us at privacy@freeonlinetools.example.
