❓ Random Picker FAQ

🎲 Is the random selection truly fair and unbiased?

Yes! We use the browser's cryptographic pseudo-random number generator (`crypto.getRandomValues`). Every person on your list has an exactly equal statistical chance of being chosen.

👥 What if the number of people does not divide evenly into teams?

The tool automatically distributes remaining participants across the teams to keep group sizes as balanced as mathematically possible.
