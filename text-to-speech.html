❓ Text to Speech FAQ

🔊 Why do some voices sound different on different devices?

Text-to-speech uses the speech synthesis engines installed in your web browser and operating system (such as Google voices on Android/Chrome, Apple Siri voices on iOS/Safari, or Microsoft voices on Windows).

🔒 Is my spoken text recorded or uploaded?

No. All speech synthesis is processed natively on your device. Zero audio or text is sent to our servers.
