❓ PDF & File Tools FAQ

🔒 How does client-side PDF processing protect my privacy?

Traditional PDF websites require you to upload confidential legal or tax documents to their cloud servers. Our website uses WebAssembly and Web Workers (`pdf-lib`) to assemble PDFs directly inside your phone or laptop's browser memory.

📄 Is there any limit on how many files I can merge?

No artificial limits! You can merge as many PDFs or images as your local device's memory can handle, with no daily caps or watermarks.
