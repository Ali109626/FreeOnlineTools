❓ To-Do List FAQ

💾 Do I need an account to save my checklist?

No account or password is required! All tasks are securely saved into your browser's persistent `localStorage`. As long as you use the same browser, your tasks will be waiting for you.

🔒 Can anyone else see my to-dos?

Never. Your tasks reside strictly in your private local browser storage and are never uploaded to any cloud database or server.
