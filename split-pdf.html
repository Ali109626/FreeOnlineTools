❓ Split PDF Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Are my confidential PDF documents safe?

Yes, 100%. The entire splitting process happens client-side inside your browser using the pdf-lib WebAssembly library. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server or stored anywhere.

✂️ Can I extract non-consecutive pages like 1, 4, and 7?

Yes! You can either type "1, 4, 7" in the page range box or tap chips 1, 4, and 7 directly. Your new PDF will only include those specific pages in the exact order selected.

📱 Can I split PDFs on Android or iPhone?

Yes. This web app works seamlessly in Chrome, Safari, Samsung Internet, and mobile browsers on both smartphones and tablets without installing any app.

📄 Does splitting reduce the quality of text or images?

Not at all. The original vector fonts, high-definition images, and layout streams are copied losslessly into the new document with zero quality degradation.
