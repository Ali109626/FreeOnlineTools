❓ Color Picker FAQ

🎨 What is an Analogous color palette?

Analogous colors sit next to each other on the 360° color wheel. They create serene, natural, and visually comfortable designs often found in nature.

💉 Can I sample colors from other windows or tabs?

Yes! On Chromium-based browsers (Chrome, Edge, Opera), the "Pick from Screen" button activates the native EyeDropper API to sample any pixel anywhere on your display.
