🪙 Nisab Threshold Reference

Nisab is the minimum qualifying wealth held for one full lunar year. The traditional threshold is 87.48 grams (7.5 tola) of Gold or 612.36 grams (52.5 tola) of Silver. Most contemporary scholars recommend calculating against the Silver Nisab threshold for maximum charity benefit.

❓ Zakat Calculator FAQ

🪙 Is personal property like houses or personal cars subject to Zakat?

No. Primary residences, personal vehicles, everyday household clothing, and furniture are exempt from Zakat. Only wealth held for growth, trade, gold, silver, and liquid savings are zakatable.

📅 When is Zakat paid?

Zakat is due once every lunar (Hijri) year (354 days) after your wealth has maintained or exceeded the Nisab threshold for that entire duration. Many Muslims choose to fulfill this duty during Ramadan.
