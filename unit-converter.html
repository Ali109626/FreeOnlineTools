❓ Unit Converter FAQ

🌡️ How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit manually?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 1.8 (or 9/5) and add 32. For example, 25°C × 1.8 + 32 = 77°F.

💾 How many Megabytes are in 1 Gigabyte?

In standard decimal computing (SI standard), 1 Gigabyte (GB) = 1,000 Megabytes (MB). In binary memory (Gibibytes), 1 GiB = 1,024 MiB.
