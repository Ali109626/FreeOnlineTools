❓ Calculators FAQ

⚡ Can I change the unit slab rates in the Electricity Bill Calculator?

Yes! Electricity tariffs change periodically in Pakistan. We provide standard base rates, but you can freely edit every single slab rate and surcharge in our interactive table to reflect your exact monthly DISCO tariff.

🪙 What is the Nisab threshold for Zakat?

The traditional Nisab is equivalent to 87.48 grams (7.5 tola) of gold or 612.36 grams (52.5 tola) of silver. If your net zakatable wealth equals or exceeds this threshold for one lunar year, Zakat of 2.5% is due.
