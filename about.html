About FreeOnlineTools

Built on the conviction that everyday online tools should be fast, completely free, and genuinely respect user privacy.

Our Mission

Most web tool suites force users to create accounts, wait in slow server upload queues, navigate aggressive popups, or accept watermarks on their output. Worse yet, uploading sensitive personal documents, financial statements, and private family photos to unknown third-party cloud servers presents substantial privacy vulnerabilities.

FreeOnlineTools was created to change that paradigm. By leveraging modern client-side web technologies—including the HTML5 Canvas API, Web Speech API, Web Audio API, and WebAssembly—all calculations, image manipulations, and PDF assemblies are executed 100% locally inside your browser. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server.

Core Design Principles

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: What happens on your device stays on your device. Zero cloud uploads, zero data mining.
  • Mobile-First & Ultra-Fast: Handcrafted HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript without heavy JavaScript frameworks, ensuring snappy performance even on budget Android smartphones and slower mobile networks.
  • No Watermarks, No Subscriptions: Every tool is unrestricted and completely free forever. No registration or credit cards required.
  • Tappable & Accessible: Generous button targets (min 44px) and high-contrast, clean typography designed for ease of use.

Our 7 Tool Categories

We provide 31 carefully engineered utilities across seven distinct categories:

  1. Image Tools: Compressor, Resizer, Converter (JPG/PNG/WebP), Cropper.
  2. Text Tools: Word & Character Counter, Roman to Urdu transliteration, Text to Speech, Case Converter, Duplicate Line Remover.
  3. Calculators: Islamic Zakat Calculator, Pakistan Electricity Bill (LESCO/MEPCO/K-Electric) Calculator with editable slab tariffs, Age Calculator, Discount & Percentage, and BMI.
  4. PDF & File Tools: Merge PDFs and Images to PDF converter using browser-side pdf-lib.
  5. Generators: Downloadable QR Code generator, Cryptographic Password generator, Color Palette generator, and Random Name/Team picker.
  6. Fun & Utility: LocalStorage To-Do list, Stopwatch & Timer with audio synthesis, Unit Converter, and PKR Currency Converter with editable exchange rates.
  7. Finance Tools: Salary Tax Calculator, EMI/Loan Calculator, Compound Interest Calculator, Savings Goal Planner.
